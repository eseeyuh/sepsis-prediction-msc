AUROC: 0.82 (val) / 0.84 (test) — хороший, реалистичный результат
Это прямо в диапазоне того, что публикуется в литературе по этому бенчмарку для baseline-моделей с feature engineering (обычно 0.80–0.85 без глубокого тюнинга). Значит твой pipeline данных, признаки и модель работают корректно — не завышенно (что было бы подозрительно и намекало на утечку данных) и не заниженно.
AUPRC: 0.11 — выглядит низко, но это ожидаемо, и вот почему
AUPRC нужно всегда читать относительно базовой частоты положительного класса, а не в абсолютных цифрах как AUROC. Сепсис — редкое событие: положительных часов в этом датасете обычно около 1.5–2% от всех patient-hours. Если бы модель предсказывала случайно, AUPRC был бы примерно равен этой базовой частоте (~0.015–0.02). У тебя — 0.11, то есть примерно в 6-7 раз лучше случайного. Это стоит явно написать в диссертации именно так — не просто указать цифру 0.11, а сравнить с случайным базовым уровнем, иначе экзаменатор может неверно её прочитать как "низкую".
Utility score: 0.47–0.48 — это самое важное число здесь
В отличие от игрушечных данных (где было 0.0), здесь модель реально "переходит" порог 0.5 в клинически значимые моменты, и получает почти половину максимально возможной пользы между полным бездействием и идеальными предсказаниями. Это хороший, правдоподобный результат — публикуемые Challenge-модели показывали нормализованный utility в похожем диапазоне (топовые решения были около 0.36–0.43 по официальной шкале, но у нас чуть другая нормализация, поэтому сравнивать напрямую нельзя, важен порядок величины — и он совпадает).

"Threshold-invariant calibration methods (temperature scaling) left decision-level metrics unchanged by construction, since they preserve the sign of the logit around the 0.5 threshold. Flexible calibration methods (Platt scaling, isotonic regression) achieved near-perfect calibration (ECE reduced from 0.26 to <0.002) but caused utility at the conventional 0.5 threshold to collapse toward the inaction baseline — an expected consequence of class imbalance: honestly calibrated probabilities for a rare event rarely exceed 0.5. Selecting an operating threshold on the validation set (maximising normalised Challenge utility) and applying it to the held-out test set recovered strong utility performance, demonstrating that threshold selection is a necessary, deliberate step after calibration, not a default to leave unexamined."
Это ровно то, что рубрика называет "critical engagement with the subject matter" — не просто прогнать методы, а понять и объяснить почему результат получился именно таким.

Правильная формулировка, а не "calibration improved results":
"Calibration substantially improved the honesty of predicted probabilities (ECE: 0.26 → 0.001) without materially changing discrimination (AUROC: 0.837 → 0.836) or utility at an optimally-tuned threshold. This indicates that, for this metric, threshold selection — not calibration — is the dominant factor in recovering usable performance from a rare-event classifier. However, calibration remains essential as a prerequisite for the later phases of this work: conformal prediction (Phase 4) and decision curve analysis (Phase 6) both require probability estimates that are meaningful in absolute terms, not merely well-ranked."
Это именно тот тип находки, который рубрика описывает как "critical engagement" — ты не просто прогнал методы и взял лучшую цифру, а честно показал, что именно каждый компонент даёт, и признал, где ожидания не подтвердились. Это сильнее, чем если бы калибровка "магически" всё улучшила.

"Both LAC and APS nonconformity scores achieved coverage close to their nominal targets, confirming the theoretical guarantee holds empirically regardless of score choice. However, the two methods expressed model uncertainty in structurally different ways: LAC, which thresholds raw probability magnitude, produced zero double-inclusion ('ambiguous') sets across all tested alpha values, instead expressing uncertainty as empty sets — a consequence of calibrated sepsis probabilities rarely approaching 0.5 under severe class imbalance. APS, which ranks classes rather than thresholding magnitude directly, was able to produce genuine {no sepsis, sepsis} sets in the same regime. This suggests APS may be the more clinically interpretable choice for this task, since an empty set has no natural clinical reading, whereas a double-inclusion set maps directly onto 'defer to clinical judgement'."

"On the real test set (n=3687 patients), LAC produced zero double-inclusion sets across all tested alpha values (0.05, 0.1, 0.2), instead expressing uncertainty exclusively through empty sets (2.9%–17.9% of hours, increasing with alpha). APS produced a modest but non-zero rate of genuine {no sepsis, sepsis} ambiguous sets, most pronounced at the strictest coverage level (2.74% of hours at alpha=0.05), decreasing as alpha loosened. Both methods achieved empirical coverage within 2 percentage points of their nominal target at every alpha tested, confirming the coverage guarantee holds empirically under both nonconformity scores. This supports selecting APS as the primary uncertainty-quantification method for clinical deployment scenarios, where an interpretable 'defer to clinician' signal is preferable to an empty prediction set with no natural clinical reading."

"Stratified faithfulness analysis revealed that SHAP explanations substantially outperformed a random-ranking baseline on both deletion AUC (0.460 vs. 0.579) and insertion AUC (0.743 vs. 0.597) for high-risk instances — the cases where a clinician would actually see an alert and rely on the explanation. On the remaining, confidently-negative majority of instances, SHAP did not clearly outperform the random baseline, consistent with a floor effect: when the predicted probability is already near zero, there is limited room for feature removal to further reduce it regardless of which features are removed. This finding supports concentrating explainability evaluation — and, by extension, clinical deployment of explanations — on high-risk predictions specifically, rather than treating faithfulness as uniform across the full range of model outputs."
Это ровно то, что рубрика описывает как "critical review" — ты не просто запустил метод и получил число, а показал, где именно метод работает, почему он не работает в другом режиме, и сделал из этого методологический вывод.

"Contrary to the hypothesis raised in Phase 5 — that HospAdmTime and Unit1's high SHAP importance reflected hospital-A-specific administrative artifacts — cross-site evaluation showed the opposite pattern: both features' importance increased under shift (ratios of 1.48 and 1.12 respectively), while Lactate's importance decreased slightly (ratio 0.86). No feature's importance collapsed toward zero. This suggests these administrative features may encode genuine, transferable information about patient trajectory and acuity rather than being spurious site-specific shortcuts. However, stable or increased SHAP magnitude does not by itself confirm the feature is being used appropriately under shift — it is equally consistent with the model relying more heavily on a feature whose relationship to the true outcome has itself shifted, which SHAP magnitude alone cannot distinguish. This is noted as a limitation and a natural direction for further work (e.g. partial dependence analysis on the out-of-distribution set)."

"The post-calibration reliability diagram shows near-perfect alignment with the diagonal across the low-probability range, where the vast majority of test instances lie, consistent with the near-zero ECE (0.0017–0.0048) reported numerically. Visible instability in the sparse high-probability tail (bins containing few instances, given the rarity of sepsis) reflects small-sample noise in that region rather than a genuine calibration failure — since ECE is a bin-size-weighted average, these sparse bins contribute negligibly to the overall metric."
